**Sizzle & Serve — Ember Lane Kitchen** — an original top-down cooking game in the browser. You are the chef of a tiny kitchen on Ember Lane: orders land on the board with a ticking timer, and it is on you to run the room. Grab a plate, pull buns, patties and vegetables from the pantry, chop tomato, lettuce and onion on the board (hold E), sear patties on the grill and lift them off before they burn, stack the dish on your plate, and ring the bell at the serve window before the order expires. Serve the exact recipe and the coins stack up — serve fast in a row and the combo multiplies the payout; serve the wrong dish or let an order expire and you lose a heart. Three hearts and the shift is over; survive the full 150-second shift and the kitchen is yours. Six recipes, a grill that punishes dawdling, a chopping board that rewards planning, synthesized WebAudio chops, sizzles and dings, pause/mute, and a best score kept in the browser. All art, sounds and names are original, drawn in code with canvas 2D — no image or audio files, no network requests, fully offline.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — WASD or arrows to move, E to use a station (hold E to chop), P to pause, M to mute, Enter to start.
